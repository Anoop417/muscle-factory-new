Weekly schedule

Progressive overload roadmap (4+1 week cycle)

  • Weeks 1–2 — Accumulate: work in the lower half of each rep range and leave about 3 reps in reserve. Build the habit of logging every set.
  • Weeks 3–4 — Intensify: push toward the top of each rep range with about 1–2 reps in reserve. Apply the overload rule whenever every set reaches the top rep count.
  • Week 5 — Deload: keep the same exercises but do about half the sets at roughly 60% of the working weight. Recover, then restart the cycle with the new weights.
  • Always: rest as listed, warm up before main lifts, and use the Log Workout tab, which tells you the next weight and reps to aim for.

Hydration tips

  • Drink a glass of water when you wake up.
  • Have about 250 ml before each meal.
  • Sip 150–250 ml every 15–20 minutes during training.
  • Drink more on hot days or after a hard session.
  • Pale yellow urine is a good sign that you're hydrated.
